# Judge a performance route on the combined candidate, and compare stage time when the text differs

Two measurement rules from the ADR-067 DFlash2 stage work (Qwen3.6 and Qwen3.8, four-agent workload, M5 Max):

1. Single-switch results are not additive. On Qwen3.6 the staged draft alone and the live tiles alone left the four-lane cycle at the control's 51-52 ms and the attention tile alone brought it to 42 ms; together they reached 33.6 ms. Decide whether a route earns its place by measuring the combined candidate against the combined candidate with that one switch removed, not the route alone against the old baseline.

2. Two arms that emit different text are running different workloads. These campaigns repeat one deterministic conversation per arm, so their confidence intervals cover timing noise only. Between such arms, stage time per cycle is the comparable quantity; makespan is one draw. Example: the prepared output head shortened the four-lane target stage by 3.27% while makespan did not move, because its text, cycle count and acceptance changed.
