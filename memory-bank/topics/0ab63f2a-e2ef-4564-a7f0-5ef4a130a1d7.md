# Qwen3.6 four-agent repair workload fails the workflow oracle in about one run in twelve

On the Qwen3.6 four-agent repair workload, twenty interleaved rounds (`qwen36-int-isolate-v1`, binary `d5f48657`, mlx-native 0.21.8 sources) passed 18 of 20 control runs and 19 of 20 candidate runs. All three failures were the workflow oracle's "initial six-step workflow not followed"; the transcripts are coherent tool calls that take a wrong turn, not corrupted output. The model fails this oracle at about that rate with or without the new DFlash2 routes.

What to do under these conditions:

- Do not conclude a quality regression, or its absence, from a few runs. An earlier six-block campaign was invalidated by two of six candidate failures, and five runs per switch (5/5, 5/5, 4/5, 4/5) did not separate the arms.
- Compare against an interleaved control of the same size.
- A release campaign on this workload has to budget runs for this failure rate.

The rate is measured for this model and workload only.
