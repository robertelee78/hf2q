## Unconditional Q5 host bound and units

ADR-063 `docs/adr/ADR-063-apple-silicon-four-agent-speed-coherence.md:521` requires the stricter existing family ceiling and physical memory minus at least 12 GiB system reserve, subtracting observed competing nonreclaimable use. Require `B <= F` when F exists, and independently `B+C+R <= P`. Thus `cap = min(F, P-R-C)` with F, otherwise `P-R-C`. All terms are checked integer bytes; `GiB=1,073,741,824` bytes. `R>=12,884,901,888` bytes. On exactly 128 GiB (137,438,953,472 bytes), zero-competition host cap with minimum reserve is 124,554,051,584 bytes (116 GiB), and every byte of C reduces that host cap. For DeepSeek the family cap and zero-competition host cap coincide on that host, but their scopes remain independent.

P must be actual physical RAM, C fresh aggregate external competing nonreclaimable use excluding the candidate once, and R declared OS/system headroom. Do not substitute startup free percentage or largest competitor for C; do not subtract C twice via an available-memory value. Projection B needs complete disjoint owned peak categories and transition overlap. This inventory provides no value for B or C and therefore no positive admission verdict.
